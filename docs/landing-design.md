# Pharmacy First public site design

Reviewed 16 October 2026. These are design references, not business affiliations.

## References

- [Cencora United Kingdom](https://www.cencora.com/global-reach/europe/united-kingdom): inspected the rendered page, headline and body typography, utility navigation, prominent business portal access, and large brand-color sections.
- [PHOENIX UK](https://www.phoenixmedical.co.uk/en/): inspected the rendered page, strong headline hierarchy, readable explanatory copy, and dedicated portal access.

The Pharmacy First page uses its own branding, wording, images, product illustrations, and layout. No competitor logos, claims, statistics, or licensed brand fonts are included.

## Typography

- Display, section headings, navigation and buttons: **Manrope**, variable weights 200–800.
- Body, product information, stock tables and public credentials: **Source Sans 3**, variable weights 200–900.
- Desktop headings: approximately 39–60 px. Main explanatory text: 17–21 px. Compact information and controls use smaller sizes where needed.
- Mobile main copy: 16–17 px. Responsive heading sizes and spacing preserve readable line lengths.
- Fonts are bundled as Latin WOFF2 files and loaded by `next/font/local` only on the public showcase route. The original CRM retains Inter.
- License notices are in `public/fonts/Manrope-LICENSE.txt` and `public/fonts/Source-Sans-3-LICENSE.txt`.

## Visual system

Deep green hero and footer, a white navigation bar, pale neutral product surfaces, consistent action styling, and a larger, more readable type hierarchy. Demo product and stock disclosures remain visible. Navigation, search, category filters, product dialogs, stock previews and original CRM access remain available.
